Common questions about pricing

How is the price calculated?
The price follows the stitch count of the finished design, not the size of the garment, and the larger the design, the less you pay per stitch. Our typical prices by stitch count are listed on this page. Before any charge, we send you a fixed quote within 2–4 business hours of receiving your artwork — no obligation. Designs with gradients, fine lettering or many color changes can cost more than the typical price.
What's included in the price?
All embroidery file formats (DST, PES, EMB, JEF, EXP, VP3, and more), unlimited free revisions, format conversion, and 2 years of design archive — all included with no extra fees.
Do you offer volume discounts?
Yes. If you regularly send multiple designs per month, we offer custom pricing. Contact us with your typical volume and we'll set up a discounted rate.
How quickly can you turn around a design?
Standard turnaround is 24 hours from approval. Need it faster?
