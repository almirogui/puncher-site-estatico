Common questions about pricing

Why don't you publish a detailed price list?
Every design is unique. Stitch count, size, fabric type, and artwork complexity all affect the final price. Publishing a fixed table would mean either overcharging simple designs or undercharging complex ones. Instead, we send you an exact quote within 2–4 business hours of receiving your artwork — no obligation.
What's included in the price?
All embroidery file formats (DST, PES, EMB, JEF, EXP, VP3, and more), unlimited free revisions, format conversion, and 2 years of design archive — all included with no extra fees.
Do you offer volume discounts?
Yes. If you regularly send multiple designs per month, we offer custom pricing. Contact us with your typical volume and we'll set up a discounted rate.
How quickly can you turn around a design?
Standard turnaround is 24 hours from approval. Need it faster? Add RUSH Delivery (+40%): order by 2:00 PM EST and receive your design the same day by 6:00 PM EST; after 2:00 PM EST, next morning by 8:00 AM EST. If we miss the deadline, the RUSH fee is waived.
