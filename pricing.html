Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about pricing

Why don't you publish a detailed price list?
Every design is unique. Stitch count, size, fabric type, and artwork complexity all affect the final price. Publishing a fixed table would mean either overcharging simple designs or undercharging complex ones. Instead, we send you an exact quote within 2 hours of receiving your artwork — no obligation.
What's included in the price?
All embroidery file formats (DST, PES, EMB, JEF, EXP, VP3, and more), unlimited free revisions, format conversion, and 2 years of design archive — all included with no extra fees.
Do you offer volume discounts?
Yes. If you regularly send multiple designs per month, we offer custom pricing. Contact us with your typical volume and we'll set up a discounted rate.
How quickly can you turn around a design?
Standard turnaround is 24 hours from approval. We also offer rush service (4 hours or less) for urgent projects at a special rate.
