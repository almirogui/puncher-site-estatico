The frustrations of outsourcing digitizing

You need files that run clean on the machine — first time, every time. But finding a digitizer who delivers consistently is harder than it should be.

Inconsistent quality

Freelancers disappear or their work varies wildly. One design runs great, the next breaks thread every 500 stitches.

Slow turnaround

Your customer needs the order tomorrow. Your digitizer takes 3 days — if they respond at all.

Poor communication

No updates, no previews, no answers. You end up chasing your digitizer instead of running your business.

Endless revisions

Files that need rework cost you machine time, thread, fabric, and your customer's patience.
